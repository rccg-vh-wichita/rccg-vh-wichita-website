# Design Direction

## Visual thesis

A warm editorial parish welcome: deep evergreen, chapel cream, sunlit gold, candid photography, and restrained African Pentecostal energy.

## Content plan

1. Welcome and Sunday invitation.
2. Practical service and visit information.
3. Vision, history, and leadership.
4. Ministries, events, and community impact.
5. Worship, resources, and giving.
6. A clear invitation to visit.

## Interaction thesis

- A composed hero entrance establishes presence without delaying access to content.
- Scroll reveals make the editorial rhythm feel intentional while respecting reduced-motion preferences.
- Image crops and depth shifts create atmosphere; text links and arrows provide crisp affordance.
- The mobile navigation becomes a simple full-screen table of contents with large tap targets.
